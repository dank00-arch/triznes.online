# SMILES — partner invitations, angel support, VR and financial design

Canonical product name: **SMILES**, no digit 3. Historical 3 SMILES documents remain archived but must not be used for release naming.

Master specification: https://docs.google.com/document/d/18oY1xdYqSfTkUPIYOEZ3uWCJF75Bp8w7tynMgBUtGIc/edit

## End-to-end flow
Dream → consent-based APOLLON plan → 13 stages (names to verify) → 14-day optional Sochi journey → verified event invitation → optional angel support → real creative action → consented film → next step.

## Event acceptance
Partner creates an event with capacity, address, time and coordinator; participant opts in to share limited profile; partner explicitly confirms; only then UI says 'you are expected'. QR attendance check is separate. Never share private fears, dreams or GPS by default.

## Angel support
Differentiate gift, payment for service and regulated investment. Payment provider, ledger, receipts, refund states and legal review required. Angel does not control player's private data or filming.

## VR
Optional immersive stationary experience; never direct a player to walk through traffic with opaque headset. Mobile map and voice instructions must work without VR.
