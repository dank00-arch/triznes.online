## Product modules
Public cinema/news/knowledge; auth/profile/cards; avatar; dream journal; AI assistant; map; quests; community; verified events/partners; operators/media vault; cinema; three investment/support pools; payments (only after legal review); moderation; admin; XR; iOS/Android mobile apps.

## App information architecture
Tabs: Мир | Карта | Мой путь | Кино | Профиль. Role-based operator, partner, angel and editor dashboards. Preserve public access to cinema and knowledge.

## Release gates
1. Inventory existing code, schema and approved media. Do not duplicate app.
2. Staging Supabase, backup, RLS, auth and paid-card regression tests.
3. Implement avatar/dream/map/knowledge with genuine states and consents.
4. Verified events, community moderation, operators and media permissions.
5. Legally reviewed financial pools and digital purchase rules.
6. Decide mobile stack after existing frontend audit; prepare signed iOS and Android builds.
7. TestFlight / Google Play testing; privacy policy, account deletion, UGC report/block, accurate permissions, billing, screenshots, support and review metadata.
8. Release only after passing tests and developer-account authorization.

**Not yet complete:** no verified native builds, developer account access, store submission or store approval. The original canonical 13 stages, final media shortlist and partner contracts still need verification.
