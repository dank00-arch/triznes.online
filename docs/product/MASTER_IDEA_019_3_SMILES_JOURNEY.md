Status: concept, not deployed. Preserve existing auth, paid cards, users and production.

## Flow
Public cinema and knowledge → login → opt-in photo-to-cartoon character → dream journal with optional fears and doubts → APOLLON co-designed steps → real/game Sochi map → quests → private media diary → consented premiere → next goal.

## Safety and product invariants
- No diagnosis, psychological profiling or promise to treat inner blocks. Voluntary self-description and editable AI summaries only.
- GPS opt-in and private by default; denial never blocks the app.
- Restaurant, hotel, barter and operator availability only when confirmed.
- Video recording and publication require separate consent.
- User photo, dreams, exact location and media protected by owner-scoped RLS. AI secrets server-side.
- The 13 stage names in the master document are provisional until verified against the original 13 РАСКРЫТИЕ.
- Keep original media archive; reject low-quality images from release manifest rather than delete.

## Delivery order
P0: avatar, dream journal, knowledge, private map, manual quests.
P1: verified 13 stages, user-controlled planner, goal progress.
P2: operator workflow, private media, cinema editorial review.
P3: confirmed partner availability, 14-day routes, live/catalog.

## Acceptance
iPhone and desktop; GPS-denied fallback; RLS cross-user negative tests; no publishing without consent; no invented partner booking; existing paid cards preserved; rollback.
