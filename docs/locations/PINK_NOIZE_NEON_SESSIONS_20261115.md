## Positioning
An immersive, welcoming neon creative salon for artists, actors, entrepreneurs, podcasters and community guests: “Не зритель — персонаж. Не показ — раскрытие.” The goal is real conversation and creation, not objectifying performers or pressuring guests into intimacy.

3. CHARACTER NIGHT: voluntary playful quests, introductions and collaborations; consent-first, no humiliation, forced pairing or sexual expectation.
