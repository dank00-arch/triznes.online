# SOCHI.OS v2 — approved visual direction

## Visual target
- Exact spirit of the approved concept image: immersive living Sochi, not a dashboard.
- Two worlds share the same structural interface:
  - WORLD 01 / CYBER / ИГРА — night Sochi, neon cyan/magenta/red, data density, locked zones, quest signals.
  - WORLD 02 / ABUNDANCE / РАЙ — day Sochi, sea/greenery/white glass/coral/gold, air, real people and opportunities.
- Player moves through WORLD 01 toward WORLD 02; the transition is part of the story, not merely a theme toggle.

## Functional rule
Preserve all working triznes.online navigation and mechanics. Do not invent users, profiles, chats, balances, rewards, streams, or characters. Empty states must be honest and populated only from real platform data.

## Primary shell
1. Full-bleed live Sochi map/photo layer is always visible.
2. Left navigation remains compact and persistent.
3. Right BEZLIKI panel is context-aware and shows the next real step.
4. Bottom city feed shows real locations/events/communities/stories when available.
5. Profile, chats, characters, streams, communities, pools, cards, map, music and real-estate modules remain reachable.
6. APOLLON is founder/admin orchestration and registration control; BEZLIKI is the player-facing navigator.

## Data hygiene
- PLAYER UNKNOWN before real registration.
- No fake Lvl values.
- No fake balances or passive-income counters.
- No Unsplash people as pretend members.
- If media is missing, use landscape/abstract/placeholders with no invented identity.

## Mobile
Map-first full-screen experience. Bottom dock: WORLD / QUEST / BEZLIKI / STORY / PLAYER.
